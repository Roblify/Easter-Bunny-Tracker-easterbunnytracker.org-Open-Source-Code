About

The Easter Bunny Tracker is an interactive, real-time tracking experience that follows the Easter Bunny's journey as he prepares for and delivers Easter baskets around the world. Designed to be informative, fun, and easy to follow, the tracker provides live updates, location progress, and journey statistics during the Easter season.

Development of the Easter Bunny Tracker began in 2025, with the goal of creating a modern, map-based tracker inspired by traditional holiday tracking experiences. After a year of development, testing, and refinement, 2026 marked the tracker's first official year of live Easter Bunny tracking.

The tracker is built to balance realism with imagination, offering users a way to follow the Bunny's progress without revealing too much too early. Certain details are intentionally introduced over time to preserve the excitement and mystery of the journey.

The Easter Bunny Tracker is intended for entertainment and seasonal enjoyment and is updated annually to improve accuracy, performance, and presentation.

Settings

All changes are saved automatically.

📷 Camera

Toggle between a cinematic side-tracking view and the default overhead camera while locked to the Bunny.

Only applies when "Lock to Bunny" is enabled.

Side Tracking Offset: 30°

0° = directly behind  ·  90° = side view  ·  180° = looking from the front.

Cinematic Zoom: 6
🗺️ Map

Switch between a flat 2D Mercator map and a 3D globe with atmosphere and star effects.

Switch between the Standard and Satellite map styles.

🎵 Audio

Toggle background music. Some browsers may require a page interaction before audio can start.

📊 HUD

Choose whether travel speed is shown in miles per hour or kilometres per hour.

Streamer Mode hides the "Bunny arrives at your location in about…" estimate, preventing viewers of your broadcast from inferring your location.
